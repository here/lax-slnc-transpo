B Line connections

The B Line (Metro subway) runs under Vermont Ave, just west of the NC. Each station lists only the bus stops at the same corner (within about 150 m, straight line). Metro names a stop by the street the bus runs on, then the cross street, so "Vermont / Sunset" and "Sunset / Vermont" are different stops on different sides of the intersection.
