# Aviary and Silver Lake transportation evidence baseline

**Historical baseline · November 25, 2026, Los Angeles time**

Aviary tracks observed bus speeds, road speeds and traffic incidents on selected Los Angeles corridors. The Silver Lake Neighborhood Council transportation site tracks selected City 311 requests and maps scheduled transit and street connections. This baseline records what each system had observed or published at the time of review and which corridor questions remain open.

### What the archive supports for delay

**Road travel time above free flow.** A provisional diagnostic in minutes per modeled traversal subtracts the time to traverse the nominal length at the provider’s free-flow speed from archived current travel time. The inputs may represent different links or polls. Across the pinned November section and corridor flow rows, **13,727 of 14,169 TomTom rows** and **6,175 of 6,826 HERE rows** had numeric inputs. In the five-day availability screen, Pico had numeric pairs in 720 of 720 scheduled TomTom section–direction–hour cells and 470 of 480 HERE cells. At 80% or more reported coverage and zero reported closure, 555 of 720 TomTom and 454 of 480 HERE cells qualified. The denominators cover three sections, two directions and 120 TomTom or 80 HERE scheduled hourly buckets. They measure hourly output availability. The follow-up audit found that even rows with one timestamp, 100% reported coverage and zero reported closure cannot establish that current and free-flow speeds use the same links. The number of truly paired observations is unknown. A validated delay column requires both travel times on the same deduplicated open intervals within each canonical poll. Poll availability also needs its own inventory. Provider baselines differ, and these aggregates lack traffic volumes for estimating vehicle-hours of delay. [Pinned flow data](https://github.com/here/aviary/tree/6636c6c76ec2aba9797838a69bf7546cae6bc4ba/data) · [Pinned traffic rollup](https://github.com/here/aviary/blob/6636c6c76ec2aba9797838a69bf7546cae6bc4ba/pipeline/build_traffic.py)

**Bus timetable deviation.** Match each observed arrival or departure to the scheduled event for the same trip instance, service day and stop occurrence. Actual minus scheduled time is positive for late events and negative for early events. The archive yielded **zero verifiable matched stop events**. Offline matching tests used synthetic event data and did not measure lateness. The eligible stop-event denominator is unknown. A measured deviation needs contemporaneous schedules and validated actual events. Trip updates can contain predictions, including for past stops. [Pinned corridor observation method](https://github.com/here/aviary/blob/6636c6c76ec2aba9797838a69bf7546cae6bc4ba/pipeline/build_observed_corridor.py) · [GTFS Schedule reference](https://gtfs.org/documentation/schedule/reference/) · [GTFS Realtime reference](https://gtfs.org/documentation/realtime/reference/)

**TomTom incident maximum.** The pinned rollup records the maximum numeric nonzero disruption estimate reported for a matched incident, section and direction. TomTom reports incident disruption in seconds relative to free flow and excludes road closures from the field definition. All **760 numeric rows out of 764** are jams, covering **536 of 540 distinct provider IDs**. Three road-closure rows and one road-works row are blank. Current reduction drops explicit zero alongside missing values. The full provider estimate is copied to each matching row. Summaries can deduplicate by provider ID and take its maximum, and avoid summing estimates across rows or IDs. Polling cadence and geometry filters limit detection, and the time of the stored peak is unavailable. ## Methods and limits

The review checked both repository SHAs and workflow steps, compared deployed and pinned files where indicated, and inventoried timestamp fields by dataset. It distinguished event or provider time, collection or upload time, hourly rollup bucket start, and deploy time. Row counts depend on the source's unit of observation. A row can represent a directional segment, a bus route and segment, a provider incident assigned to multiple sections, or a 311 request. Coverage should use all eligible scheduled slots in the denominator and count missing outputs as missing. Cal-ITP measured history at the pin reaches July 15, 2026. Static GTFS describes planned service, and current vehicle-position streams describe sampled movement. Each has its own time coverage and is useful for different questions.
